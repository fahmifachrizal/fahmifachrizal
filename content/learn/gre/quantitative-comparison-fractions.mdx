<Question>
Given that $x$ is a real number, compare the two quantities.

**Quantity A:** $x^2 + 2$

**Quantity B:** $2x$
</Question>

<Choices answer="A">
<Choice label="A">Quantity A is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="B">Quantity B is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="C">The two quantities are equal</Choice>
<Choice label="D">The relationship cannot be determined from the information given</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Compare by looking at the difference">
Instead of comparing $x^2 + 2$ and $2x$ directly, subtract one from the other: $(x^2 + 2) - 2x = x^2 - 2x + 2$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Complete the square">
$x^2 - 2x + 2 = (x - 1)^2 + 1$. A square is never negative, so $(x - 1)^2 \geq 0$ for every real $x$, which means $(x - 1)^2 + 1 \geq 1$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Draw the conclusion">
Since $(x^2 + 2) - 2x \geq 1$, the difference is always strictly positive — there's no value of $x$ that makes it zero or negative. So $x^2 + 2 > 2x$ for every real $x$, and **Quantity A is greater**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
