<Question>
A rectangle has a length of 12 and a width of 5. What is the length of its diagonal?
</Question>

<Choices answer="B">
<Choice label="A">12</Choice>
<Choice label="B">13</Choice>
<Choice label="C">14</Choice>
<Choice label="D">15</Choice>
<Choice label="E">17</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Apply the Pythagorean theorem">
The diagonal splits the rectangle into a right triangle with legs 12 and 5, so the diagonal $d$ satisfies $d^2 = 12^2 + 5^2$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Compute">
$d^2 = 144 + 25 = 169$, so $d = \sqrt{169} = 13$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
