<Question>
Given that $a > b > 0$, compare the two quantities.

**Quantity A:** $\frac{a}{b}$

**Quantity B:** $\frac{b}{a}$
</Question>

<Choices answer="A">
<Choice label="A">Quantity A is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="B">Quantity B is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="C">The two quantities are equal</Choice>
<Choice label="D">The relationship cannot be determined from the information given</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Subtract to compare">
Quantity A $-$ Quantity B $= \frac{a}{b} - \frac{b}{a} = \frac{a^2 - b^2}{ab}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Examine the sign of each factor">
Since $a > b > 0$, both $a$ and $b$ are positive, so $ab > 0$. Also $a > b > 0$ means $a^2 > b^2$, so $a^2 - b^2 > 0$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Draw the conclusion">
A positive numerator divided by a positive denominator is always positive, so $\frac{a}{b} - \frac{b}{a} > 0$ for every valid $a$ and $b$. Quantity A is always greater, choice **A**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
</QuestionLayout>
