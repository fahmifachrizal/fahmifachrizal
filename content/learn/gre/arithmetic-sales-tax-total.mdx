<Question>
An item is marked at \$150 before tax. If the sales tax rate is 8%, what is the total price paid, including tax?
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">\$150</Choice>
<Choice label="B">\$154</Choice>
<Choice label="C">\$158</Choice>
<Choice label="D">\$162</Choice>
<Choice label="E">\$168</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Compute the tax amount">
8% of \$150 is 0.08 × 150 = \$12.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Add the tax to the price">
Total price = \$150 + \$12 = \$162.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Double-check with a single multiplication">
Equivalently, 150 × 1.08 = 162, confirming the total is **\$162**, choice **D**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
