<Question>
In triangle $ABC$, $AB = 5$, $BC = 8$, and angle $B = 60°$. What is the length of $AC$?
</Question>

<Choices answer="B">
<Choice label="A">6</Choice>
<Choice label="B">7</Choice>
<Choice label="C">8</Choice>
<Choice label="D">9</Choice>
<Choice label="E">10</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Apply the Law of Cosines">
$AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2 - 2 \cdot AB \cdot BC \cdot \cos(B)$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Substitute values">
$AC^2 = 5^2 + 8^2 - 2(5)(8)\cos(60°) = 25 + 64 - 80 \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = 89 - 40 = 49$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Solve for AC">
$AC = \sqrt{49} = 7$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
