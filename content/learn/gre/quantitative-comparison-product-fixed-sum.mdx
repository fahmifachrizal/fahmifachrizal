<QuestionLayout>
<QuestionPane>
<Question>
$x$ and $y$ are positive integers such that $x + y = 10$.

**Quantity A:** $xy$

**Quantity B:** $24$
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">Quantity A is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="B">Quantity B is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="C">The two quantities are equal</Choice>
<Choice label="D">The relationship cannot be determined from the information given</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Try values near the middle">
If $x = 5$ and $y = 5$: $xy = 25$, which is greater than 24.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Try values further apart">
If $x = 1$ and $y = 9$: $xy = 9$, which is less than 24.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Try a value that gives equality">
If $x = 4$ and $y = 6$: $xy = 24$, which equals Quantity B exactly.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={4} title="Draw the conclusion">
Since $xy$ can be greater than, less than, or equal to 24 depending on the choice of $x$ and $y$, the relationship cannot be determined from the information given, choice **D**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
</QuestionLayout>
