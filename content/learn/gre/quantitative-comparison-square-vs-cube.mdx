<QuestionPane>
<Question>
Given that $x$ is a nonzero real number, compare the two quantities.

**Quantity A:** $x^2$

**Quantity B:** $x^3$
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">Quantity A is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="B">Quantity B is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="C">The two quantities are equal</Choice>
<Choice label="D">The relationship cannot be determined from the information given</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Try a value greater than 1">
If $x = 2$: Quantity A $= 4$ and Quantity B $= 8$, so Quantity B is greater.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Try a negative value">
If $x = -2$: Quantity A $= 4$ and Quantity B $= -8$, so Quantity A is greater.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Draw the conclusion">
Since the comparison flips depending on the value of $x$ (and the quantities are even equal when $x = 1$), the relationship cannot be determined from the information given, choice **D**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
</QuestionLayout>
