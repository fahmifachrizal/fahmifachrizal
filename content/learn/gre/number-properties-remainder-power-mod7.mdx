<Question>
What is the remainder when $3^{45}$ is divided by $7$?
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">1</Choice>
<Choice label="B">2</Choice>
<Choice label="C">3</Choice>
<Choice label="D">6</Choice>
<Choice label="E">4</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the cycle of powers of 3 mod 7">
$3^1 = 3$, $3^2 = 2$, $3^3 = 6$, $3^4 = 4$, $3^5 = 5$, $3^6 = 1 \pmod 7$, then the pattern repeats with period $6$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Reduce the exponent modulo the cycle length">
$45 = 6 × 7 + 3$, so $45 \equiv 3 \pmod 6$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Apply the cycle">
$3^{45} \equiv 3^3 \equiv 6 \pmod 7$, choice **D**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
