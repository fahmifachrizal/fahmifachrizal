<Question>
An investment earns simple interest at an annual rate of $4\%$. After $3$ years, the investment has earned \$360 in interest. What was the original principal?
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">\$1,800</Choice>
<Choice label="B">\$2,400</Choice>
<Choice label="C">\$2,700</Choice>
<Choice label="D">\$3,000</Choice>
<Choice label="E">\$3,600</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Recall the simple interest formula">
$I = P × r × t$, where $I$ is interest, $P$ is principal, $r$ is the annual rate, and $t$ is time in years.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Substitute the known values">
$360 = P × 0.04 × 3 = P × 0.12$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Solve for P">
$P = \frac{360}{0.12} = 3{,}000$, choice **D**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
