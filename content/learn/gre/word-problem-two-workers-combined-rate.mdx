<Question>
Worker A can complete a job alone in $6$ days. Worker B can complete the same job alone in $12$ days. Working together at their respective constant rates, how many days will it take them to complete the job?
</Question>

<Choices answer="B">
<Choice label="A">3</Choice>
<Choice label="B">4</Choice>
<Choice label="C">6</Choice>
<Choice label="D">8</Choice>
<Choice label="E">9</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Write each worker's rate">
Worker A's rate is $\frac{1}{6}$ job per day, and Worker B's rate is $\frac{1}{12}$ job per day.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Add the rates">
Combined rate: $\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{12} = \frac{2}{12} + \frac{1}{12} = \frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}$ job per day.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Find the time">
Time $= \frac{1}{\text{rate}} = \frac{1}{1/4} = 4$ days.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
