<Question>
When positive integer $n$ is divided by 7, the remainder is 4. What is the remainder when $3n$ is divided by 7?
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">2</Choice>
<Choice label="B">3</Choice>
<Choice label="C">4</Choice>
<Choice label="D">5</Choice>
<Choice label="E">6</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Express n in terms of the remainder">
Since $n$ divided by 7 leaves remainder 4, $n = 7k + 4$ for some non-negative integer $k$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Multiply by 3">
$3n = 21k + 12 = 7(3k + 1) + 5$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Read off the remainder">
Since $7(3k+1)$ is divisible by 7, the remainder when $3n$ is divided by 7 is 5.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
