<Question>
A circle has radius 6. What is the area of a sector of this circle with a central angle of 60°?
</Question>

<Choices answer="B">
<Choice label="A">$3\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="B">$6\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="C">$9\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="D">$12\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="E">$18\pi$</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the area of the full circle">
The area of the full circle is $\pi r^2 = \pi (6)^2 = 36\pi$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Find the sector's fraction of the circle">
A $60°$ sector is $\frac{60}{360} = \frac{1}{6}$ of the full circle.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Compute the sector area">
$\frac{1}{6} \times 36\pi = 6\pi$, choice **B**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
