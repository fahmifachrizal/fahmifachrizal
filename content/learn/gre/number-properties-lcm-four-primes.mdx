<Question>
What is the smallest positive integer that is divisible by $2$, $3$, $5$, and $7$?
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">35</Choice>
<Choice label="B">70</Choice>
<Choice label="C">105</Choice>
<Choice label="D">210</Choice>
<Choice label="E">420</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Recognize the problem as finding the LCM">
Since $2$, $3$, $5$, and $7$ are all distinct primes, the smallest positive integer divisible by all of them is simply their product.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Multiply the primes">
$2 × 3 × 5 × 7 = 210$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Confirm no smaller value works">
Any integer divisible by all four primes must contain each as a factor, so $210$ is the least such integer, choice **D**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
