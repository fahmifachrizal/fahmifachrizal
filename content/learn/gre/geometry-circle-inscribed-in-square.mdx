<Question>
A circle is inscribed in a square with side length $8$, touching all four sides. What is the area of the circle?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">$8\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="B">$12\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="C">$16\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="D">$32\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="E">$64\pi$</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the circle's diameter">
Since the circle is inscribed in the square and touches all four sides, its diameter equals the side length of the square: $8$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Find the radius">
Radius $= \frac{8}{2} = 4$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Compute the area">
Area $= \pi r^2 = \pi (4)^2 = 16\pi$, choice **C**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
