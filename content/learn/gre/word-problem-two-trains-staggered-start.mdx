<Question>
Two trains start from stations 300 miles apart and travel toward each other. Train A travels at 60 mph, Train B travels at 40 mph, and Train B departs 30 minutes after Train A. How long after Train A departs do the two trains meet?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">2.8 hours</Choice>
<Choice label="B">3 hours</Choice>
<Choice label="C">3.2 hours</Choice>
<Choice label="D">3.5 hours</Choice>
<Choice label="E">4 hours</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Define the time variable">
Let $t$ be the number of hours after Train A departs. Train A travels for $t$ hours, and Train B, which leaves 0.5 hours later, travels for $t - 0.5$ hours.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Set up the distance equation">
The trains meet when their combined distance equals 300 miles: $60t + 40(t - 0.5) = 300$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Solve for t">
$60t + 40t - 20 = 300$, so $100t = 320$, giving $t = 3.2$ hours.
</SolutionStep>
