<Question>
A jacket is originally priced at $50. It is discounted by 20%, and then 8% sales tax is applied to the discounted price. What is the final price paid, in dollars?
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">40.00</Choice>
<Choice label="B">41.60</Choice>
<Choice label="C">42.00</Choice>
<Choice label="D">43.20</Choice>
<Choice label="E">44.00</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Apply the discount">
A 20% discount means the customer pays 80% of the original price: $50 \times 0.8 = 40$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Apply the sales tax">
The 8% tax is applied to the discounted price of $40, so the final price is $40 \times 1.08$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Compute the result">
$40 \times 1.08 = 43.20$, so the final price is **$43.20**, choice **D**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
