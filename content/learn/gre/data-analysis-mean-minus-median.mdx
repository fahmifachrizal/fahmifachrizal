<Question>
The data set $\{4, 7, 7, 9, 13\}$ has a mean and a median. What is the mean minus the median?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">-1</Choice>
<Choice label="B">0</Choice>
<Choice label="C">1</Choice>
<Choice label="D">2</Choice>
<Choice label="E">3</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the mean">
Mean $= \frac{4 + 7 + 7 + 9 + 13}{5} = \frac{40}{5} = 8$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Find the median">
The data is already sorted; the middle (3rd) value is 7, so the median is 7.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Subtract">
$8 - 7 = 1$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
