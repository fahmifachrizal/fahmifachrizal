<Question>
If $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$, what is the sum of the two solutions for $x$?
</Question>

<Choices answer="B">
<Choice label="A">1</Choice>
<Choice label="B">5</Choice>
<Choice label="C">6</Choice>
<Choice label="D">7</Choice>
<Choice label="E">11</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Factor the quadratic">
$x^2 - 5x + 6$ factors into $(x - 2)(x - 3)$, since $-2 \times -3 = 6$ and $-2 + -3 = -5$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Solve for x">
Setting each factor to zero: $x - 2 = 0$ gives $x = 2$, and $x - 3 = 0$ gives $x = 3$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Add the solutions">
$2 + 3 = 5$. (This also matches Vieta's formula: for $x^2 + bx + c = 0$, the sum of the roots is $-b$, and here $-b = 5$.)
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
