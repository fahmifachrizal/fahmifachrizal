<Question>
What is the greatest common divisor of 84 and 126?
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">6</Choice>
<Choice label="B">14</Choice>
<Choice label="C">21</Choice>
<Choice label="D">42</Choice>
<Choice label="E">84</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the prime factorizations">
$84 = 2^2 \times 3 \times 7$ and $126 = 2 \times 3^2 \times 7$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Take the shared prime factors at their lowest powers">
The common factors are $2^1$, $3^1$, and $7^1$, so $\gcd(84, 126) = 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
