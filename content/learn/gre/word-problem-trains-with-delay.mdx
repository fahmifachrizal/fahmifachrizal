<Question>
Two trains start at the same time from stations 300 miles apart and travel toward each other along the same track. Train A travels at a constant 40 miles per hour. Train B travels at a constant 60 miles per hour, but stops for a 30-minute delay exactly 1 hour after departure before resuming at the same speed. How many hours after departure do the trains meet?
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">3.0</Choice>
<Choice label="B">3.1</Choice>
<Choice label="C">3.2</Choice>
<Choice label="D">3.3</Choice>
<Choice label="E">3.5</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the distance closed in the first hour">
In the first hour, Train A covers $40$ miles and Train B covers $60$ miles, closing $40 + 60 = 100$ miles. Remaining distance: $300 - 100 = 200$ miles.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Account for the delay">
During Train B's 30-minute stop, only Train A moves, covering $40 \times 0.5 = 20$ miles. Remaining distance after the stop: $200 - 20 = 180$ miles, at elapsed time $1.5$ hours.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Close the remaining distance">
After the stop, both trains move toward each other again at a combined rate of $40 + 60 = 100$ miles per hour. Time to close $180$ miles: $\frac{180}{100} = 1.8$ hours.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={4} title="Add up the total time">
Total time $= 1.5 + 1.8 = 3.3$ hours, choice **D**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
