<Question>
The mean of a list of $5$ numbers is $20$. If the number $30$ is removed from the list, what is the mean of the remaining $4$ numbers?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">15</Choice>
<Choice label="B">16.25</Choice>
<Choice label="C">17.5</Choice>
<Choice label="D">18.75</Choice>
<Choice label="E">20</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the original sum">
Sum $= \text{mean} × \text{count} = 20 × 5 = 100$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Subtract the removed value">
Remaining sum $= 100 - 30 = 70$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Divide by the new count">
New mean $= \frac{70}{4} = 17.5$, choice **C**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
