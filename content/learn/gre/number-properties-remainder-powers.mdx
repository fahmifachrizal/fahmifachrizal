<Question>
What is the remainder when $7^{100}$ is divided by 5?
</Question>

<Choices answer="A">
<Choice label="A">1</Choice>
<Choice label="B">2</Choice>
<Choice label="C">3</Choice>
<Choice label="D">4</Choice>
<Choice label="E">0</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Reduce the base modulo 5">
Since $7 \equiv 2 \pmod{5}$, the remainder of $7^{100}$ divided by 5 is the same as the remainder of $2^{100}$ divided by 5.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Find the cycle of powers of 2 mod 5">
$2^1 \equiv 2$, $2^2 \equiv 4$, $2^3 \equiv 3$, $2^4 \equiv 1 \pmod{5}$, and then the pattern repeats with period 4.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Locate 100 in the cycle">
Since $100$ is a multiple of 4 ($100 = 4 \times 25$), $2^{100} \equiv 2^4 \equiv 1 \pmod{5}$. The remainder is **1**, choice **A**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
