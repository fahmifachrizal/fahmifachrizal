<Question>
How many positive divisors does 60 have?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">8</Choice>
<Choice label="B">10</Choice>
<Choice label="C">12</Choice>
<Choice label="D">14</Choice>
<Choice label="E">16</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the prime factorization">
$60 = 2^2 \times 3^1 \times 5^1$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Apply the divisor-counting formula">
For a number $p^a \times q^b \times r^c$, the number of positive divisors is $(a+1)(b+1)(c+1)$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Compute the count">
$(2+1)(1+1)(1+1) = 3 \times 2 \times 2 = 12$, choice **C**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
