<Question>
$1000$ is invested at a simple annual interest rate of $5\%$. What is the total amount, including interest, after $3$ years?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">$1050</Choice>
<Choice label="B">$1100</Choice>
<Choice label="C">$1150</Choice>
<Choice label="D">$1200</Choice>
<Choice label="E">$1250</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Compute the simple interest">
Simple interest $= P \times r \times t = 1000 \times 0.05 \times 3 = 150$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Add the interest to the principal">
Total amount $= 1000 + 150 = 1150$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
