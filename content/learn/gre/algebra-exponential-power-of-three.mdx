<Question>
If $3^{2x - 1} = 27$, what is the value of $x$?
</Question>

<Choices answer="B">
<Choice label="A">1</Choice>
<Choice label="B">2</Choice>
<Choice label="C">3</Choice>
<Choice label="D">4</Choice>
<Choice label="E">5</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Express 27 as a power of 3">
$27 = 3^3$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Equate the exponents">
Since $3^{2x-1} = 3^3$, it follows that $2x - 1 = 3$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Solve for x">
$2x = 4$, so $x = 2$, choice **B**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
