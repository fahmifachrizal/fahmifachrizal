<Question>
**Quantity A:** $\sqrt{50}$

**Quantity B:** $7$
</Question>

<Choices answer="A">
<Choice label="A">Quantity A is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="B">Quantity B is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="C">The two quantities are equal</Choice>

</Choices>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Square Quantity B for comparison">
Since both quantities are positive, compare their squares instead: Quantity A squared is $50$, and Quantity B squared is $7^2 = 49$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Compare the squares">
$50 > 49$, so $\sqrt{50} > 7$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Draw the conclusion">
Since this holds with no variables involved, Quantity A is always greater.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
