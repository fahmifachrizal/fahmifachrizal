<Question>
Compare the two quantities.

**Quantity A:** $\frac{3}{4}$

**Quantity B:** $\frac{7}{10}$
</Question>

<Choices answer="A">
<Choice label="A">Quantity A is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="B">Quantity B is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="C">The two quantities are equal</Choice>
<Choice label="D">The relationship cannot be determined from the information given</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Convert to a common denominator">
The least common denominator of 4 and 10 is 20: $\frac{3}{4} = \frac{15}{20}$ and $\frac{7}{10} = \frac{14}{20}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Compare">
$\frac{15}{20} > \frac{14}{20}$, so Quantity A is greater.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
