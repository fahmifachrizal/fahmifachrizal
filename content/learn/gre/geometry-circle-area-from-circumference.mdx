<Question>
A circle has a circumference of $18\pi$. What is the area of the circle?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">$9\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="B">$18\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="C">$81\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="D">$162\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="E">$324\pi$</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the radius">
Circumference $= 2\pi r = 18\pi$, so $r = 9$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Compute the area">
Area $= \pi r^2 = \pi (9)^2 = 81\pi$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
