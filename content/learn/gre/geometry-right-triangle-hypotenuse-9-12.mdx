<Question>
A right triangle has legs of length $9$ and $12$. What is the length of its hypotenuse?
</Question>

<Choices answer="B">
<Choice label="A">13</Choice>
<Choice label="B">15</Choice>
<Choice label="C">17</Choice>
<Choice label="D">18</Choice>
<Choice label="E">21</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Apply the Pythagorean theorem">
For a right triangle with legs $a$ and $b$ and hypotenuse $c$: $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Substitute the given legs">
$c^2 = 9^2 + 12^2 = 81 + 144 = 225$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Solve for c">
$c = \sqrt{225} = 15$, choice **B**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
