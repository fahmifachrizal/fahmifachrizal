<Question>
A triangle has a base of 12 and a corresponding height of 7. What is the area of the triangle?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">19</Choice>
<Choice label="B">35</Choice>
<Choice label="C">42</Choice>
<Choice label="D">56</Choice>
<Choice label="E">84</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Recall the triangle area formula">
The area of a triangle is $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Substitute the given values">
$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 7 = \frac{84}{2}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Simplify">
$\frac{84}{2} = 42$, so the area is **42**, choice **C**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
