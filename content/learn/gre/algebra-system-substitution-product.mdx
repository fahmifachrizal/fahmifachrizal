<Question>
If $x + y = 10$ and $x - y = 4$, what is the value of $xy$?
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">7</Choice>
<Choice label="B">14</Choice>
<Choice label="C">18</Choice>
<Choice label="D">21</Choice>
<Choice label="E">24</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Add the two equations">
$(x + y) + (x - y) = 10 + 4$ gives $2x = 14$, so $x = 7$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Solve for y">
Substitute $x = 7$ into $x + y = 10$: $y = 10 - 7 = 3$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Compute the product">
$xy = 7 \times 3 = 21$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
