<Question>
What is the least common multiple of $12$ and $18$?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">6</Choice>
<Choice label="B">24</Choice>
<Choice label="C">36</Choice>
<Choice label="D">72</Choice>
<Choice label="E">216</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the prime factorizations">
$12 = 2^2 \times 3$ and $18 = 2 \times 3^2$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Take the highest power of each prime">
The LCM uses $2^2$ and $3^2$: $\text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
