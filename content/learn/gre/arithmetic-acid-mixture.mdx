<Question>
A solution is 30% acid by volume. How many liters of pure acid (100% acid) must be added to 10 liters of this solution to create a new solution that is 50% acid?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">2</Choice>
<Choice label="B">3</Choice>
<Choice label="C">4</Choice>
<Choice label="D">5</Choice>
<Choice label="E">6</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Set up the acid balance">
Let $x$ be the liters of pure acid added. The acid before mixing is $0.30(10) + x$, and the total volume after mixing is $10 + x$, which must be 50% acid.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Write the equation">
$0.30(10) + x = 0.50(10 + x)$, which simplifies to $3 + x = 5 + 0.5x$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Solve for x">
$0.5x = 2$, so $x = 4$. Adding 4 liters of pure acid gives a 50% acid solution.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
