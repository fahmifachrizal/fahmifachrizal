<Question>
Two similar triangles have corresponding side lengths in the ratio $2:5$. If the area of the smaller triangle is $8$ square units, what is the area of the larger triangle, in square units?
</Question>

<Choices answer="E">
<Choice label="A">20</Choice>
<Choice label="B">32</Choice>
<Choice label="C">40</Choice>
<Choice label="D">45</Choice>
<Choice label="E">50</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Recall how area scales for similar figures">
For similar figures, the ratio of areas equals the square of the ratio of corresponding side lengths.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Compute the area ratio">
Side ratio is $2:5$, so the area ratio is $2^2 : 5^2 = 4 : 25$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Scale the smaller triangle's area">
$8 \times \frac{25}{4} = 50$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
