<Question>
A right circular cylinder has a radius of $3$ and a height of $10$. What is its volume, in terms of $\pi$?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">$30\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="B">$60\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="C">$90\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="D">$100\pi$</Choice>
<Choice label="E">$120\pi$</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Recall the volume formula">
The volume of a right circular cylinder is $V = \pi r^2 h$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Substitute the given values">
$V = \pi (3)^2 (10) = \pi \times 9 \times 10 = 90\pi$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
