<Question>
A rectangular box has edge lengths 4, 5, and 6. What is the length of the diagonal connecting two opposite corners of the box?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">$\sqrt{45}$</Choice>
<Choice label="B">$\sqrt{61}$</Choice>
<Choice label="C">$\sqrt{77}$</Choice>
<Choice label="D">$\sqrt{86}$</Choice>
<Choice label="E">$\sqrt{100}$</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Recall the space diagonal formula">
For a rectangular box with edge lengths $a$, $b$, and $c$, the space diagonal is $d = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Substitute the given edge lengths">
$d = \sqrt{4^2 + 5^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{16 + 25 + 36}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Add the squares">
$16 + 25 + 36 = 77$, so $d = \sqrt{77}$, choice **C**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
