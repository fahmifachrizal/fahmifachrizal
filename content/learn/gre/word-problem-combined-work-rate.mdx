<Question>
Machine A can complete a job alone in 6 hours, and Machine B can complete the same job alone in 3 hours. If both machines work together at their respective constant rates, how many hours will it take them to complete the job?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">1</Choice>
<Choice label="B">1.5</Choice>
<Choice label="C">2</Choice>
<Choice label="D">2.5</Choice>
<Choice label="E">3</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Write each machine's rate">
Machine A completes $\frac{1}{6}$ of the job per hour, and Machine B completes $\frac{1}{3}$ of the job per hour.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Add the rates">
Combined rate $= \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$ of the job per hour.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Find the time">
If they complete $\frac{1}{2}$ of the job per hour, the whole job takes $\frac{1}{1/2} = 2$ hours, choice **C**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
</QuestionLayout>
