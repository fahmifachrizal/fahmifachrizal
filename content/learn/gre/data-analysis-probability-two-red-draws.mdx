<Question>
A bag contains $5$ red marbles, $3$ blue marbles, and $2$ green marbles. If two marbles are drawn at random without replacement, what is the probability that both are red?
</Question>

<Choices answer="B">
<Choice label="A">$\frac{1}{5}$</Choice>
<Choice label="B">$\frac{2}{9}$</Choice>
<Choice label="C">$\frac{1}{4}$</Choice>
<Choice label="D">$\frac{1}{2}$</Choice>
<Choice label="E">$\frac{5}{9}$</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the probability the first marble is red">
There are $10$ marbles total, $5$ of which are red: $P(\text{first red}) = \frac{5}{10}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Find the probability the second marble is red">
After removing one red marble, $4$ red marbles remain out of $9$ total: $P(\text{second red}) = \frac{4}{9}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Multiply the probabilities">
$P(\text{both red}) = \frac{5}{10} \times \frac{4}{9} = \frac{20}{90} = \frac{2}{9}$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
