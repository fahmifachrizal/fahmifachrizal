<Question>
If $2^x = 32$, what is the value of $x$?
</Question>

<Choices answer="A">
<Choice label="A">5</Choice>
<Choice label="B">6</Choice>
<Choice label="C">10</Choice>
<Choice label="D">16</Choice>
<Choice label="E">32</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Express 32 as a power of 2">
$32 = 2^5$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Equate the exponents">
Since $2^x = 2^5$, it follows that $x = 5$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
