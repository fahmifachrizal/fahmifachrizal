<Question>
In an isosceles triangle, the vertex angle measures $40°$. What is the measure of each base angle?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">40°</Choice>
<Choice label="B">60°</Choice>
<Choice label="C">70°</Choice>
<Choice label="D">80°</Choice>
<Choice label="E">100°</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Recall the angle sum of a triangle">
The three interior angles of a triangle sum to $180°$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Set up the equation">
In an isosceles triangle, the two base angles are equal. Let each base angle be $b$: $40 + b + b = 180$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Solve for the base angle">
$2b = 180 - 40 = 140$, so $b = 70°$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
