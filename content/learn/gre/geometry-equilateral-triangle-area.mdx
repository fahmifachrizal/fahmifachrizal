<Question>
An equilateral triangle has a side length of $6$. What is its area?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">$6\sqrt{3}$</Choice>
<Choice label="B">$8\sqrt{3}$</Choice>
<Choice label="C">$9\sqrt{3}$</Choice>
<Choice label="D">$12\sqrt{3}$</Choice>
<Choice label="E">$18\sqrt{3}$</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Recall the equilateral triangle area formula">
For an equilateral triangle with side $s$, area $= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} s^2$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Substitute the given side length">
Area $= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} (6)^2 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} × 36$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Simplify">
$\frac{36}{4} = 9$, so the area $= 9\sqrt{3}$, choice **C**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
