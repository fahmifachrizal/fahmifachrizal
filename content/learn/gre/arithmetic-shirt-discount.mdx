<Question>
A shirt originally priced at $40 is discounted by 25%. What is the sale price of the shirt?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">$25</Choice>
<Choice label="B">$28</Choice>
<Choice label="C">$30</Choice>
<Choice label="D">$32</Choice>
<Choice label="E">$35</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the discount amount">
25% of $40 is $0.25 \times 40 = 10$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Subtract from the original price">
$40 - 10 = 30$. The sale price is $30.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
