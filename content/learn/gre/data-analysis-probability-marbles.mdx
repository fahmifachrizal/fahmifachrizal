<Question>
A bag contains 5 red marbles, 3 blue marbles, and 2 green marbles. Two marbles are drawn at random without replacement. What is the probability that both marbles drawn are red?
</Question>

<Choices answer="B">
<Choice label="A">$\frac{1}{9}$</Choice>
<Choice label="B">$\frac{2}{9}$</Choice>
<Choice label="C">$\frac{1}{5}$</Choice>
<Choice label="D">$\frac{4}{9}$</Choice>
<Choice label="E">$\frac{1}{2}$</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the probability the first marble is red">
There are 10 marbles total, 5 of which are red, so $P(\text{first red}) = \frac{5}{10}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Find the probability the second marble is red">
After removing one red marble, 9 marbles remain, 4 of which are red, so $P(\text{second red} \mid \text{first red}) = \frac{4}{9}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Multiply the probabilities">
$P(\text{both red}) = \frac{5}{10} \times \frac{4}{9} = \frac{20}{90} = \frac{2}{9}$, choice **B**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
