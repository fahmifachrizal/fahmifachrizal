<Question>
What is the remainder when $7^{100}$ is divided by $5$?
</Question>

<Choices answer="A">
<Choice label="A">1</Choice>
<Choice label="B">2</Choice>
<Choice label="C">3</Choice>
<Choice label="D">4</Choice>
<Choice label="E">0</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Reduce the base modulo 5">
$7 \equiv 2 \pmod{5}$, so $7^{100} \equiv 2^{100} \pmod{5}$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Find the cycle of powers of 2 mod 5">
$2^1 = 2$, $2^2 = 4$, $2^3 = 3$, $2^4 = 1 \pmod 5$, then the pattern repeats with period $4$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Apply the cycle to the exponent">
Since $100$ is divisible by $4$ ($100 = 4 \times 25$), $2^{100} \equiv 2^4 \equiv 1 \pmod 5$.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
