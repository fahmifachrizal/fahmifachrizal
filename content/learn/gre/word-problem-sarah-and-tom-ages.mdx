<Question>
Sarah is currently twice as old as her brother Tom. In 6 years, Sarah will be 1.5 times as old as Tom. How old is Tom now?
</Question>

<Choices answer="C">
<Choice label="A">4</Choice>
<Choice label="B">5</Choice>
<Choice label="C">6</Choice>
<Choice label="D">7</Choice>
<Choice label="E">8</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Set up variables">
Let Tom's current age be $t$. Then Sarah's current age is $2t$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Write the future-age equation">
In 6 years, Sarah is $2t + 6$ and Tom is $t + 6$, and Sarah will be 1.5 times Tom's age: $2t + 6 = 1.5(t + 6)$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Solve for t">
$2t + 6 = 1.5t + 9$, so $0.5t = 3$, giving $t = 6$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={4} title="Check">
Now: Sarah 12, Tom 6. In 6 years: Sarah 18, Tom 12, and $18 / 12 = 1.5$. Confirmed.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
