<Question>
Given that $x$ is a real number, compare the two quantities.

**Quantity A:** $(x + 3)^2$

**Quantity B:** $x^2 + 9$
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">Quantity A is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="B">Quantity B is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="C">The two quantities are equal</Choice>
<Choice label="D">The relationship cannot be determined from the information given</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Expand Quantity A">
$(x + 3)^2 = x^2 + 6x + 9$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Compare the difference">
Quantity A $-$ Quantity B $= (x^2 + 6x + 9) - (x^2 + 9) = 6x$. The sign of $6x$ depends on the sign of $x$.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Test values of x">
If $x = 1$: $6x = 6 > 0$, so A is greater. If $x = -1$: $6x = -6 < 0$, so B is greater. If $x = 0$: $6x = 0$, so they're equal.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={4} title="Draw the conclusion">
Since the comparison flips depending on the value of $x$, the relationship cannot be determined from the information given, choice **D**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
