<Question>
Given that $n$ is a positive integer, compare the two quantities.

**Quantity A:** $n^2$

**Quantity B:** $2n$
</Question>

<Choices answer="D">
<Choice label="A">Quantity A is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="B">Quantity B is greater</Choice>
<Choice label="C">The two quantities are equal</Choice>
<Choice label="D">The relationship cannot be determined from the information given</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Test small values of n">
If $n = 1$: Quantity A $= 1$, Quantity B $= 2$, so B is greater.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Test another value">
If $n = 2$: Quantity A $= 4$, Quantity B $= 4$, so the quantities are equal.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={3} title="Test a larger value">
If $n = 3$: Quantity A $= 9$, Quantity B $= 6$, so A is greater.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={4} title="Draw the conclusion">
Since the comparison changes depending on the value of $n$ (B greater, then equal, then A greater), the relationship cannot be determined from the information given.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
</ExplanationPane>
</QuestionLayout>
