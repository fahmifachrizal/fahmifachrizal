<Question>
Two cars start $300$ miles apart and drive toward each other along the same road, one at a constant $55$ miles per hour and the other at a constant $65$ miles per hour. How many hours does it take for them to meet?
</Question>

<Choices answer="B">
<Choice label="A">2</Choice>
<Choice label="B">2.5</Choice>
<Choice label="C">3</Choice>
<Choice label="D">3.5</Choice>
<Choice label="E">5</Choice>
</Choices>
</QuestionPane>

<ExplanationPane>
<Explanation>
<SolutionStep number={1} title="Find the combined closing speed">
Since the cars move toward each other, their speeds add: $55 + 65 = 120$ miles per hour.
</SolutionStep>

<SolutionStep number={2} title="Divide the distance by the combined speed">
Time $= \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{rate}} = \frac{300}{120} = 2.5$ hours, choice **B**.
</SolutionStep>
</Explanation>
